About CE Labs

We are cryptographers and systems engineers who believe a security claim isn't finished until it's proven.

We are a specialized European team of cryptography engineers partnering with organizations building software where the cost of failure is absolute.

Most teams stop at code reviews and pentesting. We go further. Our engineers don’t just look for bugs; we explicitly define what “secure” means for your architecture and show that your system achieves it. We replace human guesswork with empirical evidence.

Born as a dedicated spin-off from Cryspen , we carry forward a proud tradition of high-assurance engineering. While our colleagues at Cryspen continue to pioneer software safety and verification in the AI age, CE Labs operates as a focused team dedicated exclusively to cryptography and security infrastructure.
